About LocalHarvest Boxes

Bridging the gap between small South African farms and Johannesburg households.

Our Story

LocalHarvest Boxes was founded in 2024 by Tshegofatso Maleka after noticing a clear gap in the local food market. Suburban households wanted access to fresh, locally grown vegetables and artisan products, but had no convenient way to buy directly from small farmers.

What started as a small WhatsApp group of friends ordering weekly boxes has grown into a subscription service that supports a network of small-scale farms and bakers across Gauteng. We assemble customised produce boxes and deliver them straight to subscribers’ doors — cutting out supermarket middlemen and reducing food miles.

Today we operate from a home base in Johannesburg and continue to grow through word of mouth, social media, and the trust of families who care about where their food comes from.

Our Mission

To make fresh, locally sourced food accessible and convenient for every household, while supporting small South African farmers and producers.

Our Vision

A Johannesburg where families eat fresh, local food every week — and where small farms thrive because of it.

Meet Our Farms

The growers and makers behind every box

Verdant Valley Farm

Magaliesberg, Gauteng

A family-run organic farm specialising in seasonal leafy greens, root vegetables and culinary herbs. Practising regenerative agriculture since 2018.

Sunrise Orchard

Free State border

Stone fruit, citrus and berries picked at peak ripeness. The orchard has been in the same family for three generations.

Honeycomb Bakery

Johannesburg North

Artisan sourdough, rusks and seasonal baked goods made with locally milled flour and free-range eggs.

GreenLeaf Microgreens

Midrand

Indoor micro-cultivators growing nutrient-dense microgreens and edible flowers using controlled lights, nutrients and ventilation systems.

“I started LocalHarvest Boxes because I believe every family deserves access to real food — and every small farmer deserves a fair market. We’re building that connection, one box at a time.”

— Tshegofatso Maleka, Founder
